A CLEAR RECORD HELPS

Clear details help analysis.

  1. Show the whole meal

    Include the plate and all foods with minimal obstruction.

  2. Use natural light

    Avoid dark, overexposed or blurry photos.

  3. Check actual portions

    Photo-based portions are estimates. You can correct them after analysis.
